# **Topic: Debugging- Finding and fixing the cause of error** 

- **What?** 

I learned that debugging means investigating why a program behaves incorrectly and fixing the cause. 

- **Why?** 

It helps us solve problems systematically, identify the root cause, and instead of guessing and making random changes. 

- **So what?** 

At work, this helps us prevent repeated problems and make software more reliable for users.

Today I went through a debugging topic. I learned how to investigate software problems step by step. Instead of guessing, we repeat the problem, test possible causes, and check that fix works.
